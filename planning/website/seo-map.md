# Beaverton Reptiles SEO Map

## SEO Strategy

- Prioritize local-intent and beginner-intent search over broad national ecommerce terms
- Pair transactional pages with educational reassurance
- Use city and metro modifiers naturally in headings, metadata, and structured data
- Keep title tags concise and service-led

## Internal Link Plan

- Home links to all top-level commercial pages
- Product links to use-cases, pricing, FAQ, and contact
- FAQ links back to pricing, get-started, and contact
- About links to features and contact
- Contact links to FAQ and get-started for lower-friction follow-up

## Structured Data Notes

- Sitewide:
  - `LocalBusiness` or `PetStore` with address, opening hours, phone, and social profiles
  - `WebSite` with site search target if search is implemented
- Page-specific:
  - `FAQPage` on `/faq`
  - `CollectionPage` on `/product`
  - `ContactPage` on `/contact` and optionally `/get-started`

## Content Constraints

- Do not claim exact live inventory or guarantee product availability unless operationally maintained
- Do not fabricate veterinary claims, certifications, or warranty terms
- Use local modifiers where they read naturally, not in repetitive keyword blocks
