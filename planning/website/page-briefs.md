# Beaverton Reptiles Page Briefs

## Global UX Rules

- Mobile-first layout with clear thumb-reachable CTA placement
- Every primary page must show a location trust signal: Beaverton or Portland metro context
- Every page must include a clear next step: call, visit, or submit inquiry
- Avoid promising exact live inventory unless connected to a maintained source
- Tone should feel warm, knowledgeable, and beginner-friendly without sounding childish

## Home

- Purpose: establish trust quickly and route users to products, visit details, and beginner guidance
- Primary audience: first-time buyers and local shoppers comparing specialty stores
- Core sections:
  - Hero with local positioning and two CTAs: `Visit the Store`, `Ask About Availability`
  - Category band for reptiles, feeders, habitats, and supplies
  - Why locals trust us
  - Beginner-friendly care guidance
  - Store visit essentials: hours, map cue, parking/visit reassurance
  - Final CTA
- Proof elements: local expertise, humane care promise, staff guidance, store photos placeholder

## Product

- Purpose: explain product categories and set expectations for how customers browse and ask about availability
- Primary audience: shoppers looking for animals, feeders, enclosures, and supplies
- Core sections:
  - Category overview hero
  - Featured category cards
  - Availability expectations and inquiry flow
  - Care-first merchandising standards
  - Cross-links to pricing and get-started
- Proof elements: category breadth, healthy stock standards, starter bundle guidance

## Features

- Purpose: translate shop strengths into concrete customer benefits
- Primary audience: shoppers evaluating why this store is better than generic pet options
- Core sections:
  - Hero with expertise/value framing
  - Feature grid: humane care, knowledgeable staff, curated supplies, beginner education, local convenience, trusted feeders
  - What to expect in store
  - CTA band
- Proof elements: standards language, care process, local specialization

## Use Cases

- Purpose: help different customer types self-identify and move toward the right next step
- Primary audience: first-time reptile buyers, experienced keepers, feeder-only shoppers, families
- Core sections:
  - Hero
  - Journey cards for each audience segment
  - Recommended store pathways
  - FAQ teaser
  - CTA band
- Proof elements: tailored support, setup guidance, confidence-building copy

## Pricing

- Purpose: reduce uncertainty while avoiding brittle item-level pricing promises
- Primary audience: cost-conscious shoppers and first-time buyers
- Core sections:
  - Hero explaining pricing transparency
  - Pricing ranges by category
  - What affects price
  - Starter setup expectations
  - Warranty/support note if applicable
  - CTA to contact for current availability
- Proof elements: honest ranges, no-pressure guidance, package examples

## Get Started

- Purpose: convert uncertain shoppers into qualified inquiries
- Primary audience: beginners, parents, and shoppers wanting recommendations
- Core sections:
  - Hero with simple promise
  - Short guided form
  - What happens after submission
  - Visit-vs-inquire comparison
  - Secondary phone CTA
- Form fields:
  - Name
  - Email
  - Phone optional
  - Interest category
  - Experience level
  - Budget range
  - Message
- Proof elements: response expectation, staff help, no-obligation positioning

## About

- Purpose: build emotional trust and explain the store’s care philosophy
- Primary audience: local families and skeptical comparison shoppers
- Core sections:
  - Hero
  - Store story
  - Care values
  - Staff expertise positioning
  - Community/local angle
  - CTA to visit/contact
- Proof elements: local roots, humane handling, educational mission

## FAQ

- Purpose: answer purchase and support objections efficiently
- Required topics:
  - Pricing and packaging
  - Setup and onboarding
  - Comparisons and alternatives
  - Support and warranty
- UX structure:
  - Searchable or grouped accordion
  - Contact fallback block
  - Links to pricing and get-started

## Contact

- Purpose: highest-intent local conversion page
- Core sections:
  - Hero with direct call CTA
  - Contact methods
  - Hours
  - Address and map
  - Inquiry form
  - FAQ teaser
- Required data:
  - Phone
  - Email
  - Physical address
  - Hours
  - Google Maps link/embed
  - Social links

## Privacy Policy

- Purpose: disclose data collection and contact form handling
- Content requirements:
  - What data is collected
  - How inquiry/contact data is used
  - Analytics disclosure
  - Third-party services
  - Contact for privacy requests

## Terms of Service

- Purpose: define website usage terms and content disclaimers
- Content requirements:
  - Website usage terms
  - Availability/pricing disclaimer
  - External links disclaimer
  - Content ownership
  - Contact information

## Shared CTA Matrix

- `Home`: Visit the Store, Ask About Availability
- `Product`: Browse Categories, Ask About Current Stock
- `Features`: See What Makes Us Different, Visit Us
- `Use Cases`: Find Your Best Fit, Get Advice
- `Pricing`: See Pricing Guidance, Request Current Pricing
- `Get Started`: Send My Request, Call the Store
- `About`: Meet the Store, Contact Us
- `FAQ`: Get an Answer, Contact the Team
- `Contact`: Call Now, Send an Inquiry
